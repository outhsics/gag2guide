Crops & Seeds

Seed Packs are bundles of randomized seeds, from common crops to exclusive event varieties. Press Skip when opening to stop the rolling instantly — and per an in-game tip, sprinklers boost your luck when opening packs.

How crop sell price works

Formula: base price × size scale × mutation multiplier. Single-harvest crops get a reduced mutation bonus (1 + (raw − 1) × 0.15); multi-harvest plots keep the full value.
Infinite growth: certain plants keep growing with a growth curve — and become watering/mutation immune at the top tier, locking in their state.
