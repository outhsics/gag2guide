Guilds

Guilds are a core feature in Grow a Garden 2 — team up, progress together, climb the leaderboards and earn exclusive rewards. Guilds are managed through the NPC Gilbert at the Guild Stand, right next to the Sell Stand and Props Shop in the center of the map.

Create or join

Creating a guild costs 99 Robux. Joining one is free — you just need an invite. Invites are accepted or declined through your Mailbox.

Weekly competitions

Each week features a rotating guild contest:

  • Week 1 · Highest Kg — harvest the highest-kg fruit.
  • Week 2 · Cash Crop — sell your inventory for the most Sheckles.
  • Week 3 · Biggest Carrot — heaviest carrot wins; gold mutations count ×2, rainbow ×2.5.
  • Week 4 · Big Spender — every Sheckle you spend = 1 point.
  • Week 5 · Tallest Plant — grow the tallest plant!
  • Week 6 · Pet Safari — tame wild pets: Common 5 pts, Uncommon 15, Legendary 25, Mythic 40, Super 500. Big ×3, Mega ×9, Rainbow ×3 — bonuses stack.
  • Week 7 · Leaf Rush — sell Fall Harvest inventory for the most Leaves.
Tip: mutation bonuses stack with pet-variant bonuses — time your harvests with mutations and moon events before the contest week ends.
