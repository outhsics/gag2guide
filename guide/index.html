Beginner Guide — make money fast

First 30 minutes

  • 1. Redeem every code. Free seeds and watering cans from the Codes page — a free Uncommon Seed Pack head start.
  • 2. Plant multi-harvest plots. They keep the full mutation multiplier on every harvest, unlike single-harvest crops.
  • 3. Hatch a Common Egg. ~45% combined odds of Frog or Bunny; the long shot Unicorn / Golden Dragonfly (0.22%) boosts all future mutation rolls.
  • 4. Vote on the official website. It's the source of free seed packs, and the Carrot Contest awards Legendary packs.

Money-making loop

Core loop: plant during Normal time → wait for a moon event → harvest during the event to roll weather-locked mutations → sell on the Sell Stand (crops and pets both sell).
Size pays: crop value = base × size × mutation. Water and gear up (Gear Shop) to push the size scale, then let infinite-growth plants mature fully.
Event rule: Gold / Rainbow seeds guarantee a mutation — save them for multi-harvest plots during a moon event for a double multiplier window.

Double or Nothing

The game includes a Double or Nothing wager on sells — fun, but treat it as entertainment, not a strategy.
