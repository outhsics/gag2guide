⚡ Quick answers

How do I redeem codes? Settings menu (top left) → type the code exactly → press "Claim".
Best early pets? Common Egg pulls: Frog & Bunny (~22% each) are reliable; Unicorn and Golden Dragonfly (0.22% each) boost mutation odds.
How do crops get valuable? Mutations multiply sell price — base 1% on harvest, guaranteed from Gold / Rainbow seeds.
