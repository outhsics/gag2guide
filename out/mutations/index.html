Mutations

Mutations are visual transformations that significantly increase a crop's selling price. Final value = base price × size scale × mutation multiplier.

How to get them

How do mutations work?. Each crop carries exactly one mutation at a time. Final sell value = base price × size scale × mutation multiplier. Standard crops have a base 1% chance to mutate on harvest.
Guaranteed mutations. Planting Gold Seeds or Rainbow Seeds guarantees a mutation on the grown crop — the reliable way to farm high-value produce.
Weather-locked mutations. High-tier mutations only occur during global weather cycles such as Snowfall, Lightning storms or Blood Moons. Harvest during the event to roll the mutation.
Pets that boost mutation odds. Pets like the Unicorn and Golden Dragonfly passively multiply the base mutation chances of crops in your garden.

Price math, briefly

For single-harvest crops the mutation bonus is reduced: effective = 1 + (raw − 1) × 0.15. Multi-harvest crops keep the full multiplier — long-run plots win on mutation value.
