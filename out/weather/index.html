Weather & Moons

The game runs a global time & weather cycle. Night moons are your window for rare seeds and high-tier mutations.

Notes from the wiki

The fewer people in the server, the lower the chance a gold/rainbow seed will spawn.
Certain high-tier mutations (Snowfall, Lightning, Blood Moon lines) can only be rolled during their weather event.

Fall Harvest event adds the Harvest Moon (~3.85%) alongside the standard moon table.
